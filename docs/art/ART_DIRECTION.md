# Art Direction

## Visual Identity

VOID reads as **industrial catacomb** meets **infinite abyss**: cold steel walkways, brutal shadows, and a living darkness below that never shows a floor.

The player should feel like an intruder in a facility that was never meant to be seen.

---

## Mood

- Oppressive, lonely, ancient
- Tension without constant chaos
- “Did something move down there?”
- Hostile environment, not friendly game space

Target emotion mix: **40% tension**, **30% isolation**, **20% curiosity**, **10% terror**.

---

## Color Palette

| Role | Direction |
|------|-----------|
| **Structure** | Dark greys, muted steel, charcoal stone |
| **Void** | Cold blue-black gradients, deep teal undertones |
| **Light** | Cool white / blue highlights, sparse warm accents only |
| **Gore / corruption** | Dark red, dried black, sick green-purple energy (stylized) |
| **UI** | Cold neutrals; void fall overlay = deep blue-black |

**Avoid:** neon saturation, rainbow particles, cheerful oranges filling the scene.

---

## Lighting

- **Directional** key light — hard shadows, Quake-like readability on walkable surfaces
- **Sparse** fill — never fully illuminate the pit
- **Rim / edge** lights — minimal, steel-toned
- **Abyss pulse** — subtle omni deep below (suggests the void is alive)
- Darkness must **hide** information below the play plane

---

## Materials

- Rough industrial metal and stone
- Low specular except rails and trims
- Emission used sparingly (void mist, corruption bursts, not everywhere)
- Props feel **heavy** and **abandoned**

---

## Void Treatment

The void is **not** flat black paint.

- Layered fog volumes that **move** slowly
- Upward-drifting particles in the abyss
- **No visible ground** in the pit when possible
- Gradient falloff into depth
- Rare **distant flashes** after falls (did something happen down there?)
- Corruption gas on void deaths — green/blue/purple, not cartoon fire

---

## Enemy Silhouettes

- Humanoid-readable at combat range
- Strong color read on the bridge (enemy vs player proxy)
- **Periphery observers** in the pit: static shapes that vanish when stared at
- Never fully reveal “the thing in the void” in v1

---

## UI Visual Tone

- Minimal, industrial, cold
- HUD readable during combat
- Void death messages stark and short (`PLAYER LOST TO THE VOID`)
- No playful fonts or bright arcade framing
