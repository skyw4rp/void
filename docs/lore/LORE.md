# Lore

_Lore is withheld by design. This document tracks **premise** and **mystery rules**, not a complete wiki._

---

## Premise

Humans built structures that reached too deep. Something below was not empty — it was **waiting**. The facilities failed. The void rose. What remains are kill-arenas suspended over a depth that has no measured floor.

---

## What is the Void?

- Not merely “absence of ground” — a **presence**
- Consumes matter; returns only echoes (sometimes)
- May be alive, observing, hungry, or indifferent — **never confirm in v1**
- Corruption gas and disintegration suggest the void **unmakes** rather than crushes

---

## World History

- Industrial age excavation / weapons testing (implied)
- Catastrophe erased surface connection
- Bridges and chambers float without visible support — **impossible maintenance**
- Time period deliberately vague: ancient + futuristic steel

---

## Player Role

- A fighter thrown into the system — survivor, test subject, or condemned (TBD)
- Motivation secondary to **immediate survival**
- No voice required; environmental text optional later

---

## Enemy Origin

- Other trapped combatants, constructs, or void-touched duplicates (TBD)
- Visually humanoid for **combat readability**
- Lore must not require explaining enemy type in tutorial

---

## Environmental Storytelling

- Cracks, broken pillars, hanging debris
- No living NPCs in prototype
- Future: stenciled warnings, scratched tally marks, failed lifts
- Observers in pit: are they real, memory, or void mimicry? **Do not answer.**

---

## Mystery Rules

1. **Never fully explain the void** in UI or codex v1
2. **Silhouettes and audio** imply more than text
3. Contradictory clues are allowed (unreliable world)
4. Player theories are a feature
5. Horror beats revelation
