# Business

Planning notes for VOID commercial release. Numbers and dates are placeholders.

---

## Target Platform

- **Primary:** PC (Steam)
- **Engine:** Godot 4.6
- **Future:** TBD (deck feasibility unknown until perf pass)

---

## Target Audience

- Fans of **industrial horror** (Quake, Doom mood)
- Players who enjoy **arena combat** with high stakes (ring-out)
- Horror players who prefer **atmosphere** over jumpscare streams
- Age rating expectation: **mature** (violence, void horror themes)

---

## Positioning

**One-line:** Arena combat over an infinite void — where falling is worse than dying.

**Steam short pitch:** Fight on the edge of nothing. Knock foes into the abyss or break them first — but the pit is always watching.

Differentiators:

- Void as antagonist, not backdrop
- Pit deaths as **cinematic** events
- Psychological spatial horror + readable 1v1

---

## Steam Strategy

- Page live as early as vertical slice quality allows
- Tags: Horror, FPS, Action, Indie, Atmospheric
- Capsule: silhouette on bridge, void below, minimal text
- Screenshots: pit POV, void death frame, wide empty hall (future level)
- Demo button when Phase 2 demo stable

---

## Demo Strategy

- Short session: best-of-5 match + rematch
- Single arena or two if scope allows
- End card: wishlist CTA
- Demo must show **one** strong void death and atmosphere in first 3 minutes

---

## Trailer Ideas

1. **30s:** Footstep on bridge → look down → void particles → fight → ring-out → silence → distant flash
2. **60s:** Add shield break, bazooka ring-out, observer silhouette blink
3. **No** spoiler lore; sell mood and mechanics

---

## Wishlist Strategy

- Devlog clips: void atmosphere, pit death timing
- Compare to “Quake mood + pit fighter stakes” (accurate, not misleading)
- Reddit / horror indie communities when demo-ready
- Avoid overpromising multiplayer or campaign length

---

## Community Notes

- Encourage clip sharing of void deaths and near-miss falls
- Document void death style settings for content creators
- Be clear about prototype vs demo scope in posts
- Discord / feedback form when public playtest starts
